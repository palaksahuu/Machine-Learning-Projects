1. What is ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is an AI chatbot developed by OpenAI, capable of generating human-like responses in natural language.

2. Is ChatGPT free to use?
Yes, ChatGPT is free to use. However, OpenAI offers a subscription plan called ChatGPT Plus, which provides additional features and access to GPT-4 for $20/month.

3. Can I use OpenAI’s models via API?
Yes, developers can use OpenAI’s API to integrate language models into their own applications and services.

4. How does OpenAI ensure safety?
OpenAI employs moderation tools and model fine-tuning to reduce harmful and biased outputs. Additionally, they have implemented data controls that allow users to decide how their conversations are used.

5. What are the usage limits?
Free-tier users have usage caps, while ChatGPT Plus subscribers receive general priority access and higher usage limits.
