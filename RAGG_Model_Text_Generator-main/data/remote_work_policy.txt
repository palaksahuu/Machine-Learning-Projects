1. Overview
This policy outlines the expectations, tools, and responsibilities for employees working remotely, either full-time or under hybrid arrangements.

2. Working Hours

Employees are expected to be available between 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (IST), Monday through Friday.

Flexibility is allowed with prior manager approval.

All meetings must be attended unless a valid reason is communicated in advance.

3. Tool Access & Communication

Primary tools: Slack (messaging), Zoom (video), Notion (documentation), and Jira (task tracking).

Employees must check Slack at least once every hour during work hours.

Weekly standups via Zoom are mandatory on Mondays at 10:30 AM.

4. Performance Tracking

Work progress is tracked via Jira. Tasks must be updated daily.

KPIs will be evaluated bi-weekly in 1-on-1 meetings with team leads.

Deadlines must be respected unless escalated with justification.

5. Security Protocols

Use VPN when accessing company resources.

Do not use personal devices unless authorized.

All files must be stored in the company-approved cloud system (Google Drive/Notion).

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all tools.

6. Policy Violations
Failure to adhere to this policy may lead to formal warnings or revocation of remote privileges.
